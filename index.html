House rules v2

Each mode pays out by the guess you solved on. Daily score is the four modes added up, from $40M at best to −$8M at worst. A lucky guess counts. Knowing the movie but not its name does not.

Daily ties go to more modes solved, then the best single mode. The weekly gross runs Monday to Friday and the title goes to the top gross once Friday is over. Weekend days count toward lifetime only.
